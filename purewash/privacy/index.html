This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Pure Wash: Satisfying ASMR (the “App”), a mobile cleaning game developed by Publicvoid Dev.

1. Information we collect

We do not require an account, and we do not directly ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal information to play the App. We do not operate a developer-owned database of personal player profiles.

The App may save gameplay progress, unlocked items, and settings locally on your device. This local information is used for game functionality and is not sent to a developer-operated server.

Important: Although we do not directly collect personal information for player accounts, the third-party advertising service described below may collect or process information from your device.

2. Advertising and third-party data

The App uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertisements, including banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. Watching a rewarded ad is a choice you can make to obtain an in-game benefit.

Google AdMob may automatically collect and share information such as:

  • Device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID (when available) and other app or device identifiers;
  • IP address, which can be used to estimate general location;
  • App and ad interactions, such as ad views, taps, and video engagement; and
  • Diagnostics and performance information, such as app launch information and technical performance data.

Google may use this information for advertising, ad measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention, according to its own policies and applicable law. Depending on your region, device settings, and consent choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

Learn more at Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising.

3. Advertising choices

You can manage or delete your Android Advertising ID through your device's privacy or advertising settings, where supported. If a consent or privacy-options message is required in your region, the App may provide choices through Google's User Messaging Platform. The availability of particular choices depends on your location and applicable requirements.

4. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not designed or directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. Google AdMob advertising is used in the App as described above.

5. Data security, retention, and deletion

We do not maintain a server-side database of player accounts or personal gameplay profiles. Local game progress and settings remain on your device until they are cleared through the App or device settings, or removed when the App is uninstalled, subject to your device's backup behavior.

Data processed by Google AdMob is handled under Google's security, retention, and deletion practices. You can review Google's policies using the links above. For questions about information held directly by Publicvoid Dev, contact us using the address below.

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, service providers, or legal requirements change. We will post the updated version on this page and revise its effective date.
